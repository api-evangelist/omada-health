# Omada Health

> Omada Health is a virtual-first healthcare company (founded 2011, San Francisco) delivering evidence-based chronic disease management programs — prediabetes, type 2 diabetes, hypertension, cholesterol, MSK, and GLP-1 support — to employers, health plans, and health systems. It operates as a HIPAA covered entity and business associate. Omada publishes no public developer API; this profile captures company identity, security/compliance posture, and operational transparency.
